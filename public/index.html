About Arena

Built for CSE students who want to learn fast and build real things.

Arena LPU is a student-led CSE club focused on hackathons, workshops, contests, and peer learning. The goal is simple: give students a better place to discover events, register easily, and stay active in the community.

Clear schedules, direct registration, and strong event execution for every season.
